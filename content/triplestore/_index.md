title: The LCT-01 Triplestore
---

The LCT-01 maintains a triplestore at https://github.com/a-random-lemurian/lct01-triples for structured taxonomy data. This triplestore should hopefully be the most complete repository of LCT-01 data, as one man simply does not have the labor hours available to port everything over to the *Index*.
