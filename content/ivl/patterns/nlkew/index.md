---
title: Kew (Nina Lee)
---

**Kew** is a sewing pattern by [Nina Lee](/ivl/designers/ninalee). It was released on August 18, 2017, and was the first pattern to receive comprehensive taxonomic treatment in the LCT-01, in the form of two monographs in the *Journal*.

The Kew dress has three primary views; a tea dress variant with ruched sleeves (version 1; {{< taxa kewthidae >}}); a sleeveless bodice with straps (version 2; {{< taxa helikewidae >}}), and optional off-shoulder sleeves ({{< taxa kewoidea >}}); and a skirt-only version (version 3; {{< taxa kewcasaceae >}}).
