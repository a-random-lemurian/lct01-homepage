**Kew** is a sewing pattern by [Nina Lee](/ivl/designers/ninalee). It was released on August 18, 2017, and was the first pattern to receive comprehensive taxonomic treatment in the LCT-01, in the form of two monographs in the *Journal*.

## Variants
The Kew dress has three primary views; a tea dress variant with ruched sleeves (version 1; {{< taxa kewthidae >}}); a sleeveless bodice with straps (version 2; {{< taxa helikewidae >}}), and optional off-shoulder sleeves ({{< taxa kewoidea >}}); and a skirt-only version (version 3; {{< taxa kewcasaceae >}}).

Though Nina claims that you have up to "12 possible Kew creations", if you include the Kewbellos, the skirts by themselves, and the tops by themselves, you get 19 options. (No bodice and no bottom doesn't count).

As page 7 [of the first Kew paper](https://static.lemuria.ph/lct01/pdf/2026/2026.0005_kew_dress.pdf) said, here's the table:

| - | **Sundress** | **Cold-shoulder** | **Tea** | **No bodice**
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gathered** | *{{<taxa Helikewplicata>}}* | (*{{<taxa Kewplicata>}}*) | *{{<taxa Kewthicata>}}* | *{{<taxa Kewplicasa>}}*
| **Straight** | *{{<taxa Helikewisia>}}* | *{{<taxa Kewisia>}}* | *{{<taxa Kewthea>}}* | *{{<taxa Kewisicasa>}}*
| **Parabola** | (*{{<taxa Helikewparabola>}}*) | (*{{<taxa Kewparabola>}}*) | &mdash; | *{{<taxa Kewparacasa>}}*
| **Portobello** | (*{{<taxa Helikewbellia>}}*) | &mdash; | (*{{<taxa Kewbellothea>}}*) | non-Kew
| **No bottom** | *{{<taxa Trunkewia>}}* | &mdash; | *{{<taxa Kewpepla>}}* | non-Kew

A matrix of Kew species. An emdash (&mdash;) indicates that a specific skirt&ndash;bodice combo has not been described so far, while parentheses indicate that, while the genus name has been coined, no species have been described.
