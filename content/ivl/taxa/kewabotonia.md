***Kewabotonia*** is a genus of Kew dresses, primarily characterized by the lack of a ventral medial row of buttons that are present amongst the Kewidae, and the presence of a dorsal zipper, also not present in Kewidae. It consists of three species, for each make showcased on Nina Lee's blog.

Kewabotonia's placement in Kewoidea is on phylogenetic grounds, which overrides Kewoidea's required ventral medial button row.

## Species
