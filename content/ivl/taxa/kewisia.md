***Kewisia*** is a genus of off-shoulder Kew dresses with bilateral single straps. It maps most closely to version 2 of the Kew pattern itself. The cold shoulder (the defining feature of {{<taxa Bilourida>}}) was on-trend when the Kew was released in 2017.
