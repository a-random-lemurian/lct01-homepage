The genus ***Aveniria*** encompasses the [Avenir jumpsuit](https://fridaypatterncompany.com/products/avenir-jumpsuit-pdf-pattern) from the Friday Pattern Company.

*Aveniria* is *incertae sedis* in Occlumanubrium.

## Species

**Type species**: *Aveniria panifica* Lemuria, 2026; wb. Stanley-Davies, 2021 ([view on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/CSEHxcyq34e/)). The species epithet *panifica*, meaning "female baker" in Latin, was selected because Liz Stanley-Davies goes by "The baker that sews" on YouTube.
