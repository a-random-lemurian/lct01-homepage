*Incertae sedis* in {{<taxa Occlumanubrium>}}.

**Eleanoridae** is a family of dresses, incertae sedis in Occlumanubrium, encompassing the Eleanor pattern from Deer & Doe. The dress encompasses two primary variants; one of which features two ventral medial oval cutouts.

The Eleanor is named for the grandmother of Amy, one of Closet Core's employees. It also bears phonetic similarity to the name of Eléonore Klein, one of the original founders of Deer & Doe.

The Eleanor may be polyphyletic as the ventral medial keyholes expose the manubrium; Occlumanubrium synapomorphically requires a covered manubrium.
