**Kewidae** is a family of off-shoulder dresses based on the Kew pattern by Nina Lee. It is part of the order Biligamentum.

Originating from a pattern, Kewidae species vary greatly in color and fabric, while sharing very similar physical morphology. Ventrally, Kewidae species feature a row of chest buttons positioned medially, beginning immediately inferior to the neckline and terminating on the skirt.

Kewidae has three genera, one for each possible skirt from the Kew pattern: *Kewplicata* (gathered skirt; from Latin *plicata* ("folded")), *Kewisia* (straight skirt; from Greek ίσια (ísia), feminine form of ίσιος (ísios, "straight, level")), and *Kewparabola* (from New Latin *parabola*, due to the similar angles between the skirt and a parabola facing down).
