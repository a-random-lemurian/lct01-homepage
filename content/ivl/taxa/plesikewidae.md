**Plesikewidae** is one of the two families of off-shoulder dresses based on the [Kew dress](/ivl/patterns/nlkew) by Nina Lee. Unlike Kewidae, it is characterized by divergence from the original pattern, hence the prefix *plesi-*. Genera here include *Kewabotonia*, a buttonless variant first sewn by the Gingerthread Girl (Jen Walker), and *Florankewia*, two dresses created by the sewist [Kate Eva](https://kateevadesigns.com) as a combination of three patterns.
