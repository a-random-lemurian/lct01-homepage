**Occlumanubrium**, from *occlude* + *manubrium*, meaning "hidden manubrium", is a phylum in the kingdom Abtruncusa, encompassing garments that cover both the shoulders and the [manubrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manubrium), the top part of the sternum. Clothing such as turtle necks/polo necks, crew necks, etc. belong in Occlumanubrium.
