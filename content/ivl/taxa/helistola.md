**Helistola** (from ἥλῐος *hḗlĭos* "sun" + Latin *stola* "dress") is a class encompassing sundresses. Helistola is characterized by the presence of bilateral shoulder straps, the lack of a sleeve (which distinguishes it from its closest relative Umerostentida), and wide necklines.

Despite the name, Helistola does not require taxa to be dress-length; this means that tank tops, associated more with men, belong in Helistola, under the strictly morphological criteria, alongside pinafores, camisoles, etc.

## Division
Much of Helistola's division was historically based on origin from specific pattern designers, as contrasted with the more morphological classification of other classes.

Helistola is now undergoing full reclassification, pending an eventual monograph.
