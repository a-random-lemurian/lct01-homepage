**Nina Chang-Smith** (née **Chang**) is an English pattern designer and sewist. Her interest in sewing originated from her mother teaching her basic sewing in childhood; by her middle teenage years, she began dressmaking in earnest. In 2017, she launched her pattern label, Nina Lee.
