Getting a link to this website in your logs? We'll explain.

## How we use your content
First and foremost, the LCT-01 does not run open-ended autonomous crawlers.

We fetch your content to archive and incorporate it into the LCT-01, a system of garment classification developed by Lemuria as part of his research. Feel free to read the rest of this site to learn more about it.

We do not use the content we gather or scrape to train any AI systems.

## `robots.txt`
We will respect your site's robots.txt directives. Our fetching is always human-directed and finite; we will never turn a single URL into an open-ended crawling session.

Regardless, if you have any concerns, locate Lemuria's email address on the root page of this website and make them known.

## Our user-agents
We use browser UAs when using an actual browser and the Internet Archive's UAs when we are submitting pages to its Save Page Now service.

Our custom tooling will always include a link to `https://lct01.lemuria.ph/guide/scrapers` (this page).

## Speed
We rate-limit requests on a per-site basis and use randomized delays between requests.
